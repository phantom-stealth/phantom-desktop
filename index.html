How is Phantom different from GoLogin and Multilogin? +

Phantom overrides the root Navigator.prototype and Screen.prototype with native bytecode signatures and authentic PCI Device IDs (NVIDIA RTX 4090/4070, Apple GPU, etc.). Additionally, Phantom includes a 31-target proxy verifier, SimpleLogin temp email engine, and native mobile AVD fleet built directly into the client at a fraction of their monthly price.
Can I use my own proxies with Phantom? +
Yes, Phantom supports all standard HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, and SOCKS5 proxies with or without username/password authentication. You can also import proxy lists in bulk or bind proxies directly to specific browser profiles. Phantom automatically performs a 31-target pre-flight check before connecting to prevent burned IP addresses.
How does the 31-Target Proxy Verifier work? +
The verifier executes 8 asynchronous DNSBL lookups against Spamhaus ZEN, SORBS, SpamCop, Barracuda, and DroneBL, queries IP intelligence databases for ASN, reverse DNS, and connection type (Residential vs Datacenter), probes Google Search for /sorry/ CAPTCHA flags, tests Cloudflare Turnstile, and benchmarks response latencies across 13 major social platforms and 16 dating networks.
What is the SimpleLogin Temp Email integration? +
Phantom integrates directly with SimpleLogin via REST API to create disposable aliases on your custom domains or SimpleLogin domains. When an activation email arrives from Tinder, Discord, X, or Google, Phantom automatically parses the message body and presents the 4-8 digit OTP code in a 1-click copy badge.

Yes. Both the NSIS installer and portable executable check the public distribution repository (phantom-stealth/phantom-desktop/releases/latest) automatically. When an update is published, Phantom downloads the new package silently in the background and prompts you with a 1-click relaunch.
